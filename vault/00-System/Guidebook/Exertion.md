### Exertion

Exertion is the character’s capacity to push beyond ordinary limits. It represents fatigue, hunger, thirst and the simple will to keep going when the body or mind protests.

The total Exertion pool is calculated as:

**Resolve + Constitution + Charisma**

A character may spend 1 Exertion to add one extra die to any pool. In rare, dramatically charged moments (especially those involving an Echo) the Storyteller may allow a second point to be spent.

Exertion is replenished by rest, food and drink. The bot tracks the current pool and restores points when appropriate narrative conditions are met.

**Depletion**

When the Exertion pool reaches zero the character suffers a standing –1 to the size of every dice pool. This penalty never reduces a pool below 1 die. The character can still act; they simply act at a clear disadvantage until they recover.

> Empty Exertion is not unconsciousness. It is the point at which every action costs more than it should. Characters who continue to push while empty are living on nerve and stubbornness alone.
