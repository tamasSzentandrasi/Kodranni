## Private prep

At this stage the character record is **created** in automation (draft): initial Foundation and Skill budgets are granted so spends can be validated as you go. See [Automation — Weighing](/automation/#weighing-automation-depth).

### Concept

Write or speak a short concept. Two to five sentences is enough. Longer is usually worse.

It must contain a concrete, binding **tie to the community**. Blood, debt, marriage, sponsorship, shared loss, forced service, or something equally real. A foreigner can arrive. They cannot remain outside. If the tie is weak or abstract, the concept is rejected.

This is also where you tell the Storyteller what you actually want: the kinds of stories you hope to see, what you fear for this character, what you are willing to risk. Clarity helps the Storyteller work with your offer.

The Storyteller may reject any concept that does not fit the established frame or is brought in bad faith. That decision is final.

----------

### Foundations

All nine [Foundations](/foundations/) start at **1**.

You receive **4 free points**.

| Step | Cost |
|------|------|
| 1 → 2 | 1 |
| 2 → 3 | 2 |

Maximum is **3**. A score of **0** or **4** is possible only with Storyteller approval and a matching [Trait](/traits/).

### Skills

You receive **12 points** for [Skills](/skills/).

Costs are cumulative:

| Step | Cost |
|------|------|
| 0 → 1 | 1 |
| 1 → 2 | 2 |
| 2 → 3 | 3 |

A full rank 3 therefore costs **6**. It is possible. It is expensive.

These numbers stay **private** until The Weighing begins. Do not discuss them with the other players.

## The Weighing

The Ancestors bear witness. What follows is spoken at the table — then spent. Walk the four steps in order; each panel holds the rule, a worked beat, and a best practice.

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="w1" data-step-title="Birth Omen">
<p>Roll a <strong>d20</strong> (the <a href="/dice-mechanics/#the-omen-die-and-consequences">Omen</a> die). Only you and the Storyteller see the result.</p>
<p><strong>Points</strong> = result halved and rounded up (<strong>1–10</strong>). Spend them on Foundations under the normal costs. This is the mark under which the character was born. It is not discussed at the table.</p>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>The die shows 11 → 6 Foundation points. Mara spends 2 to raise Resolve 1→2, 2 to raise Guile 1→2, and 2 to raise Intellect 1→2. No one else at the table hears the number.</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Do not narrate the Omen as cosmic destiny at the table. It is private luck and private spend. The circle will define the person by speech, not by this roll.</div>
</div>

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="w2" data-step-title="Guiding Hand" hidden>
<p>One further Omen die is rolled for skills. The full result (<strong>1–20</strong>) becomes additional skill points, spent under the normal cumulative costs.</p>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>The die shows 9. Mara puts 6 into Negotiation 0→3 (cost 1+2+3) and banks 3 for later or into Tradecraft 0→2 (1+2). Still private until claims begin.</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Spend toward the concept you already wrote. Guiding Hand is not a second concept draft — it is fuel for the person you offered.</div>
</div>

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="w3" data-step-title="Who do we see?" id="who-do-we-see" hidden>
<p>The Storyteller turns to each character in turn and asks: <strong>“Who do we see?”</strong></p>
<p>Answer in two to four spoken sentences. No more. This is the claim you make for yourself before the Ancestors.</p>
<p>Then the others may speak. Each player may offer one claim about the person standing there: <strong>“I see ___.”</strong> — a short adjective or phrase not already in the written concept or the spoken answer.</p>
<p><strong>Rules for claims</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>A <strong>seconder</strong> is required.</li>
<li>The target and the Storyteller may jointly refuse.</li>
<li>Only one claim from any given player may be accepted about any given target. Once one is accepted, that speaker may not offer further claims on that person.</li>
<li>When a claim is accepted, <strong>two things happen</strong>:
<ol>
<li>The <strong>speaker</strong> receives one <strong>Word</strong> (spent later on <em>their own</em> character in The Wanting).</li>
<li>The <strong>Storyteller</strong> immediately applies a <strong>fitting mark to the target</strong> — skill points in a relevant <a href="/skills/">Archetype</a>, a small Foundation adjustment, a Trait, a starting <a href="/harm/">Harm</a>, or any consequence that sharpens who that person is. The goal is <strong>definition</strong>, not punishment. The speaker does <strong>not</strong> choose this mark.</li>
</ol>
</li>
</ul>
<p>The Storyteller ends the process when a full pass produces nothing further worth accepting.</p>
<div class="kod-lanes not-content" style="margin-top:0.85rem">
<div class="kod-lane kod-lane--violet">
<p class="kod-lane__title">Speaker earns</p>
<p>One <strong>Word</strong> per accepted claim — only for their own Wanting.</p>
</div>
<div class="kod-lane kod-lane--amber">
<p class="kod-lane__title">Target is marked</p>
<p>Storyteller applies definition <em>now</em>. Not a free gift from the speaker.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>Mara has spoken who she is. Cal says: “I see a hard bargainer.” A seconder agrees. Mara and the Storyteller accept. Cal banks 1 Word. The Storyteller marks Mara now: +2 into Negotiation, or Trait “Hard bargainer.” Cal does not spend on Mara.</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Speak true things you have already heard in the concept or prep talk — not jokes that waste a seconder. Definition sticks for the whole campaign.</div>
</div>

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="w4" data-step-title="The Wanting" id="the-wanting" hidden>
<p>The <strong>Words</strong> you earned by speaking true claims about <strong>others</strong> are spent here — or left unspent and <strong>lost</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Important:</strong> each Word is spent on <strong>your own character</strong>, not on the person you spoke about. The target was already marked when the claim was accepted. The Wanting is your chance to reshape <strong>yourself</strong> with the currency of what you saw in others.</p>
<p>Each Word buys one entry from the menu. You name the exact Foundations or Skills on <strong>your</strong> sheet. The Storyteller approves or denies (automation instruction on <strong>your</strong> character). Any Negative Trait cost is chosen by the Storyteller.</p>
<div class="kod-menu-grid not-content">
<div class="kod-menu-item">
<p class="kod-menu-item__buy">+1 to a named Foundation</p>
<p class="kod-menu-item__cost">Cost: remove <strong>3</strong> skill points of your choice</p>
</div>
<div class="kod-menu-item">
<p class="kod-menu-item__buy">+2 Foundation points</p>
<p class="kod-menu-item__cost">Must split across two different Foundations. Cost: remove <strong>5</strong> skill points <strong>or</strong> accept one Negative Trait (Storyteller chooses)</p>
</div>
<div class="kod-menu-item">
<p class="kod-menu-item__buy">+5 skill points</p>
<p class="kod-menu-item__cost">Cost: <strong>−1</strong> from a named Foundation of your choice</p>
</div>
<div class="kod-menu-item">
<p class="kod-menu-item__buy">One Positive named Trait</p>
<p class="kod-menu-item__cost">Cost: remove <strong>3</strong> skill points of your choice</p>
</div>
</div>
<p style="margin-top:0.75rem">No other options exist.</p>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>Cal has one Word. He buys “+5 skill points,” pays −1 Constitution on his own sheet, and names Skills the Storyteller confirms. Mara’s sheet does not change in this spend — she was already marked when the claim landed.</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Spend every Word. Unspent Words vanish. Do not hoard for a purchase that is not on the menu.</div>
</div>

## After the Weighing

Lock the following before the character enters the record:

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| **[Echoes](/echoes/)** | Drawn from accepted claims and the original concept. Community tie must be present. Capacity = **max(Strength, Dexterity) + Intellect + Authority**. **Zero Echoes = Decadence.** |
| **[Hierarchy](/hierarchies/)** | Clarify position if applicable. Default is **Outcast**. |
| **[Traits](/traits/)** | Locked. |
| **[Inventory](/inventory/)** | Partial only: named items that already make sense. No free supplies. Rest filled in play. |

Only then is the character written into the campaign record (via [automation](/automation/) when available).
