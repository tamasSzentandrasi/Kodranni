## Private prep

The character record is opened as a **draft** (automation when available): Foundation and Skill budgets are granted so spends can be checked as you go. See [Automation — Weighing](/automation/#weighing-automation-depth).

### Concept

Write or speak a short concept. **Two to five sentences.** Longer is usually worse.

It must include a concrete **tie to the community** — blood, debt, marriage, sponsorship, shared loss, forced service, or something equally solid. A foreigner may arrive; they cannot stay an outsider. Weak or abstract ties are rejected.

Also tell the Storyteller what you want from play: kinds of story, what you fear for this person, what you will risk. That helps them work with you.

The Storyteller may reject a concept that does not fit the frame or is offered in bad faith. That call is final.

----------

### Foundations

All nine [Foundations](/foundations/) start at **1**.

You get **4 free points**.

| Step | Cost |
|------|------|
| 1 → 2 | 1 |
| 2 → 3 | 2 |

Maximum **3**. A **0** or **4** needs Storyteller approval and a matching [Trait](/traits/).

### Skills

You get **12 points** for [Skills](/skills/).

Costs stack:

| Step | Cost |
|------|------|
| 0 → 1 | 1 |
| 1 → 2 | 2 |
| 2 → 3 | 3 |

Rank 3 in one Skill therefore costs **6**. Possible; expensive.

**Keep all of these numbers private** until The Weighing. Do not discuss them with the other players.

## The Weighing {#the-weighing}

Four steps. Walk them **in order**. Private rolls first; then the table speaks; then you spend what you earned — or lose it.

| Step | What happens | Who sees it |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| **1. Birth Omen** | d20 → Foundation points | You + Storyteller only |
| **2. Guiding Hand** | d20 → Skill points | You + Storyteller only |
| **3. <span id="who-do-we-see">Who do we see?</span>** | You speak; others may claim; marks and Words | Whole table |
| **4. <span id="the-wanting">The Wanting</span>** | Spend Words on **your** sheet | Whole table |

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="w1" data-step-title="Birth Omen">
<p><strong>What:</strong> Roll a <strong>d20</strong> (<a href="/omens/">Omen</a>). Only you and the Storyteller see it.</p>
<p><strong>Points:</strong> result halved and rounded up → <strong>1–10</strong> Foundation points. Spend them on Foundations at the normal costs (1→2 costs 1; 2→3 costs 2).</p>
<p><strong>Why:</strong> private luck for the character’s inborn range. Not a public prophecy. Do not discuss the number at the table.</p>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>Die shows 11 → 6 Foundation points. Mara spends 2 to raise Resolve 1→2, 2 for Guile 1→2, 2 for Intellect 1→2. No one else hears the roll.</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Spend toward the concept you already wrote. Do not re-roll for flavour. The table will define the person by speech in step 3, not by this die.</div>
</div>

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="w2" data-step-title="Guiding Hand" hidden>
<p><strong>What:</strong> Roll a second <strong>d20</strong> for Skills. The full result (<strong>1–20</strong>) is extra Skill points. Spend at normal cumulative costs (0→1 = 1, 1→2 = 2, 2→3 = 3).</p>
<p><strong>Still private</strong> until step 3.</p>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>Die shows 9. Mara puts 6 into Negotiation 0→3 (1+2+3) and 3 into Tradecraft 0→2 (1+2).</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Fuel the same concept. Guiding Hand is not a second draft of who the character is.</div>
</div>

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="w3" data-step-title="Who do we see?" hidden>
<p>This is the public half of creation. One character at a time.</p>
<p><strong>1. Self-claim.</strong> The Storyteller asks: <strong>“Who do we see?”</strong> The player answers in <strong>two to four spoken sentences</strong>. No monologue.</p>
<p><strong>2. Others claim.</strong> Each other player may offer one short claim: <strong>“I see ___.”</strong> — an adjective or phrase <em>not</em> already in the written concept or the self-claim.</p>
<p><strong>3. Accept or refuse.</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>A <strong>seconder</strong> is required for any claim to stand.</li>
<li>The <strong>target</strong> and the <strong>Storyteller</strong> may jointly refuse.</li>
<li>Only <strong>one accepted claim per speaker per target</strong>. After one lands, that speaker is done with that person.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>4. When a claim is accepted, two things happen at once:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Speaker</strong> gains one <strong>Word</strong> — spent later in The Wanting on <em>their own</em> character, not on the target.</li>
<li><strong>Storyteller</strong> applies a <strong>mark on the target now</strong>: Skill points in a fitting <a href="/skills/">Archetype</a>, a small Foundation shift, a Trait, starting <a href="/harm/">Harm</a>, or another consequence that sharpens who they are. The goal is <strong>definition</strong>, not punishment. The speaker does <strong>not</strong> choose the mark.</li>
</ol>
<p>The Storyteller ends when a full pass of the circle produces nothing further worth accepting.</p>
<div class="kod-lanes not-content" style="margin-top:0.85rem">
<div class="kod-lane kod-lane--violet">
<p class="kod-lane__title">Speaker gets</p>
<p>One <strong>Word</strong> per accepted claim — for their own Wanting only.</p>
</div>
<div class="kod-lane kod-lane--amber">
<p class="kod-lane__title">Target gets</p>
<p>A Storyteller mark <em>immediately</em>. Not a free gift chosen by the speaker.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>Mara has said who she is. Cal: “I see a hard bargainer.” Someone seconds. Mara and the Storyteller accept. Cal banks 1 Word. The Storyteller marks Mara now (+2 Negotiation, or Trait “Hard bargainer”). Cal does not spend on Mara’s sheet.</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Claim things that fit what you already heard in concept and prep — not jokes. Accepted claims stick for the campaign.</div>
</div>

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="w4" data-step-title="The Wanting" hidden>
<p><strong>Words</strong> come only from claims you made about <strong>other people</strong> in step 3. You spend them here on <strong>your own character</strong> — or leave them unspent and <strong>lose them</strong>.</p>
<p>The person you spoke about was already marked when the claim was accepted. The Wanting does not touch their sheet. It reshapes <strong>yours</strong>.</p>
<p>Each Word buys <strong>one</strong> menu option. You name the Foundations or Skills on <strong>your</strong> sheet. The Storyteller approves or denies. If a Negative Trait is required, the Storyteller chooses it.</p>
<div class="kod-menu-grid not-content">
<div class="kod-menu-item">
<p class="kod-menu-item__buy">+1 to one named Foundation</p>
<p class="kod-menu-item__cost">Pay: remove <strong>3</strong> skill points of your choice</p>
</div>
<div class="kod-menu-item">
<p class="kod-menu-item__buy">+2 Foundation points (split)</p>
<p class="kod-menu-item__cost">Must go to two different Foundations. Pay: remove <strong>5</strong> skill points <strong>or</strong> take one Negative Trait (Storyteller chooses)</p>
</div>
<div class="kod-menu-item">
<p class="kod-menu-item__buy">+5 skill points</p>
<p class="kod-menu-item__cost">Pay: <strong>−1</strong> from one named Foundation of your choice</p>
</div>
<div class="kod-menu-item">
<p class="kod-menu-item__buy">One Positive named Trait</p>
<p class="kod-menu-item__cost">Pay: remove <strong>3</strong> skill points of your choice</p>
</div>
</div>
<p style="margin-top:0.75rem">That is the full menu. Nothing else.</p>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>Cal has one Word. He takes “+5 skill points,” pays −1 Constitution on his own sheet, and names Skills the Storyteller confirms. Mara’s sheet does not change in this spend — she was already marked when the claim landed.</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Spend every Word. Unspent Words are gone. Do not invent purchases that are not on the menu.</div>
</div>

## After the Weighing

Lock these before the character enters the campaign record:

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| **[Echoes](/echoes/)** | From the concept and accepted claims. Community tie required. Capacity = **max(Strength, Dexterity) + Intellect + Authority**. **Zero Echoes = Decadence.** |
| **[Hierarchy](/hierarchies/)** | Position if any; default **Outcast**. Standing is claimed in play, not invented as a full ladder at creation. |
| **[Traits](/traits/)** | Locked. |
| **[Inventory](/inventory/)** | Only named items that already fit the concept. No free packs. The rest is found or bought in play. |

Then write the character into the record ([automation](/automation/) when available).
