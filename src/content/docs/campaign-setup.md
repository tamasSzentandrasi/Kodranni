This phase is almost entirely Storyteller work. Player input is limited to ranking concrete campaign seeds. [Character creation](/character-creation/) comes later.

If you are a player reading ahead: you only need the constraints and the seeds you are asked to rank. Everything else on this page is for the person preparing the table.

----------

## Interest Signal

Players receive only a ranked list of campaign seeds and the system constraints. Nothing else written. If someone asks about a specific name, answer in two spoken sentences at most.

<div class="kod-lanes not-content">
<div class="kod-lane kod-lane--st">
<p class="kod-lane__title">Constraints (state once)</p>
<p><strong>No magic.</strong> Pre-industrial tools and knowledge only. Ordinary human beings.<br /><strong>The community is the real protagonist.</strong> If it is destroyed, the campaign ends.<br /><strong>Death is permanent.</strong></p>
</div>
<div class="kod-lane kod-lane--player">
<p class="kod-lane__title">What players do</p>
<p>Rank your top three seeds. Ask at most two spoken sentences about any name. Wait for the Storyteller to frame the community — then create characters.</p>
</div>
</div>

The seeds below are **examples only**. Replace them freely. Players rank their top three.

A good seed uses a **settlement-scale name** — a peel, a township, a harbour, a city a person could walk to — not a country or a vague label like “river country”. It names a **community**, a **real pressure** already true this season (war, law, plague, tax, famine, migration) that can recur, and **people already pulling** — named wants that cannot all be fed. It is not a tactic menu. It is not a single lawsuit. It is not a list of horrors. It is not a predicted ending. Do not list the community’s destruction as a choice on the card. Grim reality is the weather of the age — not the product you are selling.

Write three facts. Semi-historical adds a **Break**. Players rank the card; they do not receive what named people will do if the table never sits. That next work belongs in your notes, at [Strain now](#worldbuilding-practices).

<p class="kod-seed__title">Mangerton, Liddesdale, 1323</p>
<ul class="kod-seed__facts">
<li><strong>Who</strong> — Armstrong peel-tower on the Liddel. Cattle are the wealth; they mix with the neighbours’ on the moss. Men from this tower rode with Andrew Harclay last year. Two widows of those men still eat here. A girl from this house is held hostage in a neighbouring peel for a blood-price Harclay had delayed.</li>
<li><strong>Pressure</strong> — 3 March: Harclay, Warden of the West March, is hanged at Harraby Hill as a traitor for a private peace with Robert Bruce. His following is broken. Hay is still standing. James Douglas is already lifting English herds on the Lyne. Anthony Lucy, who arrested Harclay, holds a king’s writ for the west march and will come to count what Harclay’s men held — this tower included.</li>
<li><strong>Pull</strong> — Open the peel to Lucy and be named lawful: Douglas burns the cattle as English. Send the herds into Scotland and ride with Douglas: Lucy hangs whoever is left as Harclay’s remnant. The neighbouring peel will not return the hostage until the blood-price is paid in cattle Mangerton needs for winter. The widows still claim a share of this tower’s food. None of those can all be met before the hay.</li>
</ul>

<p class="kod-seed__title">Kabar at the Aranyos salt, 899</p>
<ul class="kod-seed__facts">
<li><strong>Who</strong> — Kabar horse-people wintering above the Roman salt mines at Torda on the Aranyos. Kundaj leads them under a Khazar oath they never gave to Árpád. In the valley Slavomir’s households work the pits: they know which shafts flood, which hold, and who is still owed last year’s measure. A Kabar woman is already married into Slavomir’s house.</li>
<li><strong>Pressure</strong> — Árpád’s seven have sent riders for the salt as tribute before the host comes west. The horses cannot winter this high on grass alone. Kundaj’s men will not work the pits themselves — that is how a following becomes a line on someone else’s count. If Slavomir’s pit-hands flee or are killed, water takes the shafts and this winter’s salt is gone.</li>
<li><strong>Pull</strong> — Kundaj can take loaded wagons and leave Slavomir alive to work; the seven will still write the Kabars as Árpád’s when they arrive. Slavomir can flood one shaft to prove the mines die without him, and lose the salt his children eat. The woman’s brothers want her back before the host comes. The seven’s envoy has already named another man to sit the pits if Slavomir will not load.</li>

<p class="kod-seed__title">Kaisereia, 1072</p>
<ul class="kod-seed__facts">
<li><strong>Break</strong> — After the 1067 sack the city stood half-empty for decades. Here, one year after Manzikert, the remnant still holds Justinian’s citadel and Basil’s shrine while the empire eats itself in the capital.</li>
<li><strong>Who</strong> — Kaisereia under Mount Argaeus, on the old road toward Armenia. Greek households in the upper town; the Armenian Artsruni house, which still has a grain-store below the citadel; the Basiliad — Basil’s hospital — full of sick and poor; Nikephoros the doux, who has already sent one horse west. <li><strong>Pressure</strong> — Forage parties at the gates. Grain inside is counted in weeks. Last week’s dead from the Basiliad are unburied; the ground outside the walls is not safe. Romanos has been blinded; no column is coming. A Seljuk emir’s envoy will keep the name Kaisereia as a tax-farm if the citadel is handed over.</li>
<li><strong>Pull</strong> — Nikephoros can take the envoy’s pay and ride west; the Artsruni grain then feeds the emir’s men. The Artsruni can hold the south wall and their store and let the upper town starve first. The Basiliad’s poor will force a gate if that store stays shut. The Frank will open a postern for whoever pays. The clergy will not give Basil’s plate to buy grain. All of that is already in motion.</li>

Gudrid will offer to foster a child of the coast people, because Snorri cannot be rowed to Greenland on a ship that will not hold. The coast people will not share timber if the halls keep cutting. <p class="kod-seed__title">Aspalath, after the night the knife missed</p>
<ul class="kod-seed__facts">

The Free Company lives in a warehouse Lazzaro Orvanti signed to them twelve years ago, after they held the hill gate against Pelesa. Burghers still have the old list of families that once named a count. Pelesa is the rival harbour across a generation of galley-war. Osvaldo Calvaro is a hinterland king who wants this port as a client.</li>
<li><strong>Pressure</strong> — A knife meant for Marino killed Duchess Isotta and Lazzaro. Marino lives and cannot ride. Captain Vito’s household is writing lower-town names at night. Luca Bandi (Pelesa) and Matteo Rinaldi (Calvaro) are both already buying inside the walls, and they want opposite things.</li>

<ul class="kod-seed__facts">
<li><strong>Who</strong> — Fishing town on an inland lake. Catch is still divided by old household shares. Plague is in the town. The monastery on the island holds the remaining grain and is the only house still taking the sick.</li>
<li><strong>Pressure</strong> — The plague is not slowing. Velesin already decides who may draw monastery grain. He wants the boats pulled and the island landing barred, so the sick cannot leave and no new mouths arrive. ## Worldbuilding Practices

Do these **in order**. Prep **situations** — unstable circumstances, people with goals, places under pressure — not a fixed sequence of scenes. The story is what happens when the table acts. You may know more than you show; you do not open with a lore dump.

The worked thread below follows **Aspalath, after the night the knife missed**. Change the seed; keep the method.

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="1" data-step-title="Spectrum">
<p><strong>Lock the spectrum</strong> — tightly historical, semi-historical, or fully invented — and keep it for the whole campaign.</p>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>Fully invented: Aspalath is a ducal limestone harbour-city — olive, cisterns, salt, a staple-war with Pelesa older than anyone living. Made-up names are fine; hunger, weather, and armed neighbours still work as in history. House Orvanti, House Solari, House Calvo, the Free Company, Pelesa, and Osvaldo Calvaro are as real as any family that eats there. Do not mix an elected count into a ducal seat in the same sentence.</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Write the spectrum on the first line of your notes. Historical: real seasons, law, and armies constrain you — use that. Semi-historical: change one hinge, then honour the consequences. Invented: same gravity of hunger, weather, and power. Do not invent away the cost of living, and do not invent cruelty for spectacle.</div>
</div>

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="2" data-step-title="How they live" hidden>
<p><strong>How does this people stay alive from season to season?</strong> Work, food, shelter, who settles quarrels. Where characters “should” be when they are not on a job.</p>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>Most of Aspalath lives by the harbour and the terraces: olive press in season, salt pans in the heat, fish when the boats are not taken for a galley, cistern-water all year. Calvo stores feed the lower town between presses. The Free Company sleeps in the old Orsani warehouse inside the walls and eats what Lazzaro still owed them. The cathedral doles grain at the door, in Latin; boat families bury in their own tongue. Quarrels that used to go to a named count now go to Duke Marino’s household: Captain Vito’s men take names at night, and Paolo Cresti copies streets onto a roll. Some burghers still keep the old roll from when this city named a count. When people are not “on a job” they are at the press, the pans, the warehouses, the Company house, or the cathedral square — not at leisure.</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Material life is the foundation of the world. Know the hard season and who is free in it. If play never touches how people eat and work, the place feels hollow. Who settles quarrels is part of how they live — an office, a clerk, a roll — not a modern label with no hand. If you cannot say how they get through late winter, fix that before you invent another faction. Do not give conquerors the skills of the people they just destroyed unless play earns that change.</div>
</div>

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="3" data-step-title="What must not fail" hidden>
<p><strong>Name the concrete things whose failure ends this community as a community.</strong> Stores, rights, places, tools, bonds — countable and at risk.</p>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>This year’s oil and the cisterns. Calvo stores that feed the lower town. Duke Marino still sitting, even wounded. Isotta unburied in the cathedral. The Company’s warehouse inside the walls. Vito’s night roll — the household’s way of naming enemies. Agnese’s burgher roll — the families who still claim the right to name a count. The memory of Isotta’s Book: Aspalath families Pelesa still speaks of as if the burned register existed.</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Five to eight items. Each should be able to drive a session if threatened, stolen, bargained, or defended. Prefer things that force choices between households (who eats the last sack of grain? who holds the hill gate at night? whose children are on the night roll?). A wounded ruler can be one of these — not as backstory to recite, but as a gap everyone feels when a decision needs a name on it. A tool or a crane is not the city’s soul unless a household eats by it this season.</div>
</div>

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="4" data-step-title="Strain now" hidden>
<p><strong>One present-tense strain sentence per item.</strong> What is wrong this season. Who is pressing. What they will do this moon if nobody stops them. Several sentences. They may contradict. This is next work, not the campaign’s ending, and it does not sit on the seed card players rank.</p>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>This year’s oil is thin; household arms have already taken three men from the pans for Vito’s night work, and the press will miss them. Calvo stores still feed the lower town; Piero has a Pelesan buyer for a cargo that is still in Niccolo’s warehouse — if the palace seizes the warehouse the quarter eats from the cathedral door, which does not have the grain. Duke Marino cannot ride; orders that used to come from his mouth come from Vito’s, and people in the lower town already obey the night roll who would not have obeyed the duke last month. Isotta Solari’s body is in the cathedral; Father Lovro will not bury her until Orsa Solari agrees the rite is Solari and the household agrees the death is not an Orvanti accident they can close — until then Solari men sleep in the square. The Free Company still sleeps in the old Orsani warehouse, a season behind in pay; Luca Bandi has coin for their winter if they leave the harbour open to Pelesan hulls, Matteo Rinaldi has coin if they hold the hill gate for Osvaldo Calvaro’s charter, and Jakov Bracco has taken neither. Paolo Cresti is copying Mara Vela’s street onto the night roll; Caterina Vela’s children live on that street, and three people whose names have been on that list a week have not been seen at the pans. Agnese Orsani still holds the burgher roll from when this city named a count, and she will read it in the square if the household keeps naming streets instead of finding who put the knife in.</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Strain is a situation the players can walk into, not a monologue and not a predicted ending. Prefer deadlines, faces, and next work over adjectives. If nobody acts before the next moon, named people go to work — food, ground, or unity thins. If the sentence could sit on any map, make it local to <em>this</em> place and season. Fail: cropped slogans, a single stabbed-ruler plot that resolves the city.</div>
</div>

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="5" data-step-title="Who holds it" hidden>
<p><strong>Name only the people you need to make those strains playable, and how they are tied.</strong> Blood and household first — sister, servant of twelve years, children on a named street. Those ties do not reverse. Office, pay, and foreign gold may. A few individuals, not a cast list for a novel.</p>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>Duke Marino Orvanti, wounded, still sitting, brother of dead Lazzaro, husband of dead Isotta. Orsa Solari, Isotta’s sister: she will not let the cathedral bury Isotta as an Orvanti household accident; she wants her sister in the ground as Solari, and she wants to know whether Marino’s household let the knife through. Caterina Vela dressed Isotta for twelve years; her two children live on Mara Vela’s street, which is already on the night roll; she is household, not blood. Captain Vito takes names for Marino because Marino cannot ride. Paolo Cresti copies the roll; he eats from Vito’s table. Piero Calvo holds the stores that feed the lower town; Niccolo is his brother’s son. Agnese Orsani still holds the burgher roll; she stood in the yard when Isotta burned Pelesa’s register. Father Lovro holds the body and the door. Luca Bandi (Pelesa) and Matteo Rinaldi (Osvaldo Calvaro’s purse) rent houses; they want opposite things. Jakov Bracco sleeps in the Company warehouse; Lazzaro stood godfather to his daughter. Tomaso Fero still works the mill behind the hill gate because Lazzaro pulled him off a Pelesan bench twelve years ago and the last order Lazzaro spoke to him was to finish the counterweight engine there. If two named faces have no tie, the notes are not done.</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Prep people with leverage, goals, and ties — not ranks to recite. For each: what they want this season, what they will not forgive, what they do if left, who they are bound to. A <strong>faction</strong> is a people-group, hue-coded. Categories in this city: blood houses (Orvanti, Solari, Calvo); armed (household arms, Free Company); civic claim (those who hold the duke’s naming, those who still hold the burgher roll); foreign (Pelesa, Osvaldo Calvaro’s purse); faith (the cathedral). Dual membership only across categories: Caterina is Solari household <em>and</em> Mara Vela’s street; Jakov is Company <em>and</em> unpaid Orvanti service. Never two houses of the same category on one person unless the fiction says they changed. Fail: a list of jobs; an object as a faction; two named faces with no tie.</div>
</div>

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="6" data-step-title="Leave blanks" hidden>
<p><strong>Do not pre-solve the social map for the players.</strong> Who is trusted, who leads, who is owed — that is best discovered in play through action, rumour, and consequence. You may hold the blood map in private notes. You do not open with a lecture on who sits where.</p>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>You know Orsa will not bury until she knows whether Marino’s household let the knife through, and that Agnese will read the burgher roll if the night roll keeps naming streets. You do not tell the table the order of standing. They learn it when someone is obeyed in Marino’s name — or is not — and when a roll is read aloud or a burial is refused.</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Connections, standing, and hierarchy <em>rungs</em> emerge from fiction. Naming every rung of power up front is a lore dump. Setup names at most five hierarchy axes and leaves the rungs empty of names. A live hall that already has occupancy is <em>in play</em> — a different artifact, pointed at from <a href="/hierarchies/">Hierarchies</a>, not dumped in session zero. Hold what you need for consistency; reveal through scenes.</div>
</div>

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="7" data-step-title="What they can see" hidden>
<p><strong>Three or four visible marks of recent history</strong> — things anyone can point at without a speech. Secrets are facts, not a reveal scene.</p>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>Blood scrubbed from the cathedral floor and still in the grain of the stone; Isotta’s body behind the rood screen. Boards and nails stacked against Tomaso’s mill behind the hill gate. A night-roll copy with Mara Vela’s street on it, in Paolo Cresti’s hand. Pelesan hulls at first light across the bay. Calvaro seal-wax on a charter in Matteo’s rented house. Three empty places at the pans.</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Show, do not lecture. When players ask “what happened here?”, answer with what they can walk to and touch. Repeat marks across sessions until the table has changed them. A secret is a short fact, not a delivery script. Fail: fresco-of-the-week; a cutscene that names who paid for the knife.</div>
</div>

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="8" data-step-title="Themes and threads" hidden>
<p><strong>At least three lives already in motion.</strong> Each is a situation: a want, a next action, more than one way in. Threads may contradict. Alliances reverse. Participation is the table’s. A thread in motion is not a Foundation Myth; a Myth is a thread that already ended.</p>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>Vito’s household arms take names at night because Marino cannot ride. Paolo Cresti is already copying Mara Vela’s street onto that roll; people whose names have been on it a week have stopped being seen at the pans. That is how this city now settles who is an enemy — not a banner, an office with a clerk.<br /><br />Agnese Orsani still holds the burgher roll from when Aspalath named a count. She stood in the cathedral yard when Isotta burned Pelesa’s register of rower-families. She will read the roll in the square if the household keeps naming streets instead of finding who put the knife in. That reading would put the old houses back into the naming of ships, and take it out of Vito’s hand.<br /><br />Luca Bandi, consul for Pelesa, is buying the Company’s winter so Aspalath cannot close the harbour against Pelesan hulls. Matteo Rinaldi, Osvaldo Calvaro’s man, is buying a client charter so the same harbour chokes Pelesa. They are not allies. Jakov has not taken either purse because Lazzaro stood godfather to his daughter, who sleeps in the warehouse those purses would buy.<br /><br />Orsa will not let Lovro bury Isotta as an Orvanti accident. Tomaso still works the mill because Lazzaro’s last order to him is unfinished, and Orsa paid his mother’s burial. Piero will sell a cargo to Pelesa if Niccolo will not hold the stores for the quarter. None of these is a required path. Any can rise if the players lean that way — and each advances if they do not.</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Side stories are not clutter; they are how a campaign feels lived-in. Prep each thread as a <em>situation</em>: people with goals, pressure that worsens if ignored, more than one way in. Do not script which scenes the table must play. If they never take a thread, the world still moves; if they take it, you already know who wants what. Fail: required side quests; theme slogans; one “main plot.”</div>
</div>

<div class="kod-widget__panel" data-panel-id="9" data-step-title="Fortunes" hidden>
<p><strong>Set starting <a href="/echoes/#fortunes">Fortunes</a></strong> (Vitality, Cohesion, Surplus, Standing, Tradition) as soft 0–3 from the facts above. Store them when the campaign record is created. Early play may correct them. If you start <em>in play</em>, you may also store 0–3 <a href="/echoes/#foundation-myths">Foundation Myths</a> that pass the test below, and you name hierarchy axes (rungs empty at setup).</p>
<div class="kod-step-example"><strong>Example</strong>Vitality 1 (wounded duke, dead duchess and brother, night roll already taking hands from the pans — not half the benches empty). Cohesion 0 (private plots are the default: Orvanti household, Solari grief, unpaid Company, old burgher roll, two foreign purses). Surplus 1 (stores still move; a seized warehouse or a wasted sack is a decision). Standing 2 (Pelesa and Calvaro would not buy here if the name were a joke). Tradition 0 (nobody agrees what this city is: the duke’s night roll, the burgher roll, the Company’s warehouse, the cathedral holding the body).<br /><br /><strong>Myths, if in play.</strong> A Foundation Myth is a resolved Pivotal Echo, not a founding legend and not the present strain. It passes only if a named person did a named deed, the community joined it, the work ended, someone alive can tag a <em>kind</em> of roll because that memory still governs it, the effect is narrow, and someone alive still hates the deed or still pays for it. Civic slogans fail. The night the knife missed is strain, not a Myth.<br /><br /><em>Isotta’s Book.</em> Isotta Solari’s dowry included a Pelesan register: Aspalath boat-families listed as owing rower-service on Pelesan galleys, leftover of a war her grandfather lost. The marriage-peace with Pelesa required the book returned. She had it burned in the cathedral yard. The families written in it stood with her and would not let Lovro’s predecessor take the pages to the vestry “for keeping.” House Calvo had used those same names to claim Pelesan convoy-protection on their oil; they wanted the book in a chest, not in the fire. The pages are ash. Pelesa treats the burning as theft of a public instrument and has not sent a peace-envoy since. Aspalath treats the names as free of galley-service. Tag a roll when the work is a list, a register, or a foreign claim on a name. Free extra Exertion (pool untouched). Disadvantage on the other side when they enforce a written foreign claim inside Aspalath.<br /><br /><em>Lazzaro’s Charter.</em> Twelve years ago Pelesan marines tried the hill gate while their galleys held the harbour. The Free Company, whom Lazzaro had hired and could no longer pay in coin, held the gate. Lazzaro promised them the old Orsani warehouse inside the walls as a barracks in place of the season’s pay. Marino’s father signed rather than lose the gate. The assault ended that week; the house was handed over; Pelesa withdrew. Burghers who still hold the old roll call that house a foreign garrison. The Company still sleeps there. Tag a roll when the work is whether the Company has a right to stand in the city, or to be paid in lodging versus coin. Advantage for Company members on Arms inside the walls. Forced extra Exertion for an Orvanti household roll that tries to expel or disarm them.<br /><br /><strong>Factions</strong> are people-groups, filed in categories (in this city: Noble Houses, Foreign Influence, Estate loyalty). <strong>Tags</strong> are short ST marks on a person — a chip you can filter, two to five words, a fact. Fail: an object (“Loading crane”); a category (“Foreign gold”); a sentence. Pass: Night roll. Inner-stair keys. Unpaid. Isotta’s Book. Charter house. Calvaro charter.</div>
<div class="kod-step-best"><strong>Best practice</strong>Fortunes are community weather for every scene — PCs and NPCs. Derive them from material facts. If the board looks comfortable after a killing in the palace and a night roll in the lower town, re-check the strain list; at least one Fortune should be in Crisis when the community is eating itself. Myths: if you cannot name the deed, who joined it, how it ended, and who still hates it, it is not a Myth. Tags: if it is not a fact about a person, it is not a tag.</div>
