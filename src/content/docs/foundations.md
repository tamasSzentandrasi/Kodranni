## Foundations

**Foundations** are the **inborn potential** of each character. Barring aging, trauma, or rare Storyteller events, they do not drift session to session. They feed every **dice pool** and shape how quickly Skills improve through [Practice](/skills/#improvement).

There are nine Foundations, grouped by domain:

Each Foundation is rated on a narrow scale:

| Rating | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **1** | Below average |
| **2** | Average |
| **3** | Above average |

Values of **0** or **4** are *technically possible* — rarities for extreme cases (severe disability or legendary freaks of nature), and only with Storyteller approval and a matching [Trait](/traits/).

----------

### Where Foundations Matter

| System | Interaction |
|--------|-------------|
| [Dice pools](/dice-mechanics/#the-makeup-of-the-dice-pool) | Foundation is always part of pool size (Skills too, except Primitive actions). |
| [Skills / Practice](/skills/#improvement) | Each Skill has a ruling Foundation. Foundation 3 halves Practice thresholds; Foundation 1 doubles them. |
| [Exertion](/exertion/) | Maximum pool = **Resolve + Constitution + Charisma**. |
| [Echo capacity](/echoes/#weight-and-capacity) | Same sum as Exertion maximum. |
| [Harm](/harm/) | Each Foundation has a paired Harm track; Harm reduces *effective* Foundation. |
| [Character Creation](/character-creation/) | Start at 1; free points, Birth Omen, and The Wanting all adjust Foundations. |

<aside class="kod-example">
<p class="kod-example__scene">Smoke is filling the hall. The outer door has swollen shut in the wet. Leif has no tools and no time to fetch any — he braces and drives his shoulder into the timber to force it open. This is pure force, not carpentry: a Primitive action.</p>
<ol class="kod-example__steps">
<li><strong>Intent:</strong> force the stuck door open before the smoke thickens.</li>
<li><strong>Type:</strong> Primitive — Foundation only (<strong>Strength</strong>). No Skill die. No Practice.</li>
<li><strong>Tier:</strong> <strong>d8</strong> (nothing special favours or hinders him).</li>
<li><strong>Pool:</strong> Strength 3 → <strong>3d8</strong> (+ Omen d20).</li>
<li><strong>Result:</strong> 2 Marks — the door yields after a hard shove; he is through, coughing, a moment later than he wanted.</li>
<li><strong>Note:</strong> had this been planned work with wedges and a bar, the Storyteller might name Strength + Carpentry instead. Here the fiction is raw body against wood.</li>
</ol>
</aside>
