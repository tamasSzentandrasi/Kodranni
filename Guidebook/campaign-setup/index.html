Campaign & Character Creation

Campaign Setup

“Atropos roughly tore the thread of flourishing life.”
— Statius

This phase is almost entirely Storyteller work. Player input is limited to ranking concrete campaign seeds. Character creation comes later.

If you are a player reading ahead: you only need the constraints and the seeds you are asked to rank. Everything else on this page is for the person preparing the table.

Interest Signal

Section titled “Interest Signal”

Players receive only a ranked list of campaign seeds and the system constraints. Nothing else written. If someone asks about a specific name, answer in two spoken sentences at most.

Constraints (state once)

No magic. Pre-industrial tools and knowledge only. Ordinary human beings.
The community is the real protagonist. If it is destroyed, the campaign ends.
Death is permanent.

What players do

Rank your top three seeds. Ask at most two spoken sentences about any name. Wait for the Storyteller to frame the community — then create characters.

The seeds below are examples only. Replace them freely. Players rank their top three.

A good seed uses a settlement-scale name — a peel, a township, a harbour, a city a person could walk to — not a country or a vague label like “river country”. It names a community, a real pressure already true this season (war, law, plague, tax, famine, migration) that can recur, and people already pulling — named wants that cannot all be fed. It is not a tactic menu. It is not a single lawsuit. It is not a list of horrors. It is not a predicted ending. Do not list the community’s destruction as a choice on the card. Grim reality is the weather of the age — not the product you are selling.

Write three facts. Semi-historical adds a Break. Players rank the card; they do not receive what named people will do if the table never sits. That next work belongs in your notes, at Strain now.

Campaign seeds

Section titled “Campaign seeds”

Browse by setting type

Historical: locked place and year. Semi-historical: one clear break from a real situation. Invented: same weight as history, without dressing up misery for effect.

Historical — Semi-historical — Invented

Mangerton, Liddesdale, 1323

  • Who — A peel-township in the Liddel dale: cattle on the moss, Armstrongs of Mangerton, and a following that has answered whichever warden last rode through. Douglas men and English riders share the same grass.
  • Pressure — This March Andrew Harclay, Earl of Carlisle and Warden of the West March, is hanged, drawn and quartered at Harraby Hill for a private peace with Robert Bruce at Lochmaben. The West March has no lawful protector. Hay is not in.
  • Pull — Douglas riders will write the dale onto a Scottish roll and take the cattle as tribute. Anthony Lucy’s English following — the men who arrested Harclay — will write it onto an English one and take the same cattle as a traitor’s due. Mangerton has lived by answering whoever came last; this year answering either side makes the other a predator, and the township cannot feed both followings until the hay.

Kabar at the Aranyos salt, 899

  • Who — A Kabar war-following wintering above the Roman salt-shafts at Torda: horses, wagons, a Khazar-derived oath they still keep apart from Árpád’s seven. Kundaj leads this following. In the valley, salt-people — Slavic and Avar remnant — with a župan, Slavomir, pits, and a plough. Árpád’s host is weeks of grass away.
  • Pressure — The seven have sent for the salt as tribute. Kundaj needs it to winter the horses and to remain a people who can still say no to Árpád’s count. The shafts flood if Slavomir’s people flee or are butchered; without them no one here knows the pits.
  • Pull — Kundaj will take the next wagon as the price of the Kabar alliance and leave Slavomir’s people alive if they load. The seven’s riders will count the same salt as tribute and count the Kabars onto Árpád’s roll if they are still sitting when the host arrives. Slavomir will flood the shafts rather than see his people taken as hands for either following.

Kaisereia, 1072

  • Break — The 1067 sack left the city sparsely inhabited for decades. Here the civic body still holds the walls, one year after Manzikert, while the empire eats itself in the capital.
  • Who — The remnant in the walls: Greek households; the Armenian Artsruni house, which still holds a store in the lower town; the Basiliad’s sick and poor; a doux, Nikephoros, who has already had one horse pointed west. The shrine of Basil still stands. The roads are dead. Argaeus above.
  • Pressure — Forage parties at the gates. Grain inside is counted in weeks. An envoy from a Seljuk emir offers terms that keep the name Kaisereia as a tax and take the citadel. In the capital Romanos has been blinded; no relief is coming.
  • Pull — Nikephoros can sell the citadel and ride west on what the envoy pays; the Artsruni store would then feed the emir’s men, not the Basiliad. The Artsruni can hold their store and the south wall and let the Greek lanes starve first. The Basiliad’s poor will break a gate for grain if the store is not opened. All three are already moving.

Straumfjǫrðr, five winters held

  • Break — Historically the landing was a short camp. Here they have held five winters.
  • Who — Thorfinn Karlsefni’s landing at the fjord of currents: turf halls, a forge, Gudrid, and the child Snorri, who has known no other ground. Greenlanders who named this for plenty. The hunters who were on this coast before them use the same bird-cliffs and seal-ice.
  • Pressure — Hunt and fish have failed before. Illness in the turf. Iron almost gone. Last winter they traded milk for pelts; this winter there is less milk. A killing on the shore two summers ago was paid in cloth and has not been forgotten.
  • Pull — Thorfinn’s men will take the next bird-cliff to feed the halls if the trade is refused. Gudrid will offer a child fostered and a named gift, because Snorri cannot be rowed home on the iron they have left. The hunters will not share the cliff if the halls keep taking timber. Trade can become a marriage. A second killing cannot.

Aspalath, after the night the knife missed

  • Who — A ducal harbour-city on a limestone coast: olive, cisterns, salt, a staple-war with Pelesa older than anyone living. Duke Marino Orvanti still sits. House Solari married into that seat. The Free Company sleeps in a warehouse Lazzaro Orvanti signed over to them. Calvo stores feed the lower town. Some burghers still keep a roll from when this city named a count.
  • Pressure — A knife meant for Marino killed Duchess Isotta Solari and Marino’s brother Lazzaro. Marino lives and cannot ride. His household is copying names in the lower town. Isotta is unburied in the cathedral. The Company is a season behind in pay. Osvaldo Calvaro’s man and Pelesa’s consul are both already buying inside the walls.
  • Pull — Orsa Solari will not let Isotta be buried as an Orvanti household accident. Captain Vito is already writing Mara Vela’s street onto the night roll. Agnese Orsani will read the old burgher roll in the square if the household keeps naming streets instead of finding who held the knife. Luca Bandi is buying the Company’s winter so the harbour stays open to Pelesa. Matteo Rinaldi is buying a client charter in Osvaldo Calvaro’s name so the same harbour chokes Pelesa. Those wants cannot all be fed.

Lychnis, in the pestilence

  • Who — Net-houses on a lake that does not reach the sea. Old families still divide the catch by custom. Plague in the lanes. The island monastery is now lazaretto and last granary.
  • Pressure — The pestilence does not slacken. In the square, Velesin offers a plan: stop the burial parties that still carry the dead through the living lanes, pull the boats so no one leaves the island with the sickness, and he holds the roll of who may take grain from the monastery. Three days’ march off, the Saryn host, who say they are the Scourge of the Gods.
  • Pull — Velesin will seal the island and decide who eats from the last granary. The old families will keep burying their own through the square, because a person who is not buried is not theirs. The monastery still opens the door to the sick. If Velesin holds the roll, the Saryn arrive to a people that has already decided which of its living count. If the boats stay in the water, the sickness leaves with them and so does the grain.

Worldbuilding Practices

Section titled “Worldbuilding Practices”

Do these in order. Prep situations — unstable circumstances, people with goals, places under pressure — not a fixed sequence of scenes. The story is what happens when the table acts. You may know more than you show; you do not open with a lore dump.

The worked thread below follows Aspalath, after the night the knife missed. Change the seed; keep the method.

Worldbuilding walkthrough

Nine steps. Enough material for weight and choice. Not a novel the players must recite.

Lock the spectrum — tightly historical, semi-historical, or fully invented — and keep it for the whole campaign.

ExampleFully invented: Aspalath is a ducal limestone harbour-city — olive, cisterns, salt, a staple-war with Pelesa older than anyone living. Made-up names are fine; hunger, weather, and armed neighbours still work as in history. House Orvanti, House Solari, House Calvo, the Free Company, Pelesa, and Osvaldo Calvaro are as real as any family that eats there. Do not mix an elected count into a ducal seat in the same sentence.
Best practiceWrite the spectrum on the first line of your notes. Historical: real seasons, law, and armies constrain you — use that. Semi-historical: change one hinge, then honour the consequences. Invented: same gravity of hunger, weather, and power. Do not invent away the cost of living, and do not invent cruelty for spectacle.

How does this people stay alive from season to season? Work, food, shelter, who settles quarrels. Where characters “should” be when they are not on a job.

ExampleMost of Aspalath lives by the harbour and the terraces: olive press in season, salt pans in the heat, fish when the boats are not taken for a galley, cistern-water all year. Calvo stores feed the lower town between presses. The Free Company sleeps in the old Orsani warehouse inside the walls and eats what Lazzaro still owed them. The cathedral doles grain at the door, in Latin; boat families bury in their own tongue. Quarrels that used to go to a named count now go to Duke Marino’s household: Captain Vito’s men take names at night, and Paolo Cresti copies streets onto a roll. Some burghers still keep the old roll from when this city named a count. When people are not “on a job” they are at the press, the pans, the warehouses, the Company house, or the cathedral square — not at leisure.
Best practiceMaterial life is the foundation of the world. Know the hard season and who is free in it. If play never touches how people eat and work, the place feels hollow. Who settles quarrels is part of how they live — an office, a clerk, a roll — not a modern label with no hand. If you cannot say how they get through late winter, fix that before you invent another faction. Do not give conquerors the skills of the people they just destroyed unless play earns that change.

Name the concrete things whose failure ends this community as a community. Stores, rights, places, tools, bonds — countable and at risk.

ExampleThis year’s oil and the cisterns. Calvo stores that feed the lower town. Duke Marino still sitting, even wounded. Isotta unburied in the cathedral. The Company’s warehouse inside the walls. Vito’s night roll — the household’s way of naming enemies. Agnese’s burgher roll — the families who still claim the right to name a count. The memory of Isotta’s Book: Aspalath families Pelesa still speaks of as if the burned register existed.
Best practiceFive to eight items. Each should be able to drive a session if threatened, stolen, bargained, or defended. Prefer things that force choices between households (who eats the last sack of grain? who holds the hill gate at night? whose children are on the night roll?). A wounded ruler can be one of these — not as backstory to recite, but as a gap everyone feels when a decision needs a name on it. A tool or a crane is not the city’s soul unless a household eats by it this season.

One present-tense strain sentence per item. What is wrong this season. Who is pressing. What they will do this moon if nobody stops them. Several sentences. They may contradict. This is next work, not the campaign’s ending, and it does not sit on the seed card players rank.

ExampleThis year’s oil is thin; household arms have already taken three men from the pans for Vito’s night work, and the press will miss them. Calvo stores still feed the lower town; Piero has a Pelesan buyer for a cargo that is still in Niccolo’s warehouse — if the palace seizes the warehouse the quarter eats from the cathedral door, which does not have the grain. Duke Marino cannot ride; orders that used to come from his mouth come from Vito’s, and people in the lower town already obey the night roll who would not have obeyed the duke last month. Isotta Solari’s body is in the cathedral; Father Lovro will not bury her until Orsa Solari agrees the rite is Solari and the household agrees the death is not an Orvanti accident they can close — until then Solari men sleep in the square. The Free Company still sleeps in the old Orsani warehouse, a season behind in pay; Luca Bandi has coin for their winter if they leave the harbour open to Pelesan hulls, Matteo Rinaldi has coin if they hold the hill gate for Osvaldo Calvaro’s charter, and Jakov Bracco has taken neither. Paolo Cresti is copying Mara Vela’s street onto the night roll; Caterina Vela’s children live on that street, and three people whose names have been on that list a week have not been seen at the pans. Agnese Orsani still holds the burgher roll from when this city named a count, and she will read it in the square if the household keeps naming streets instead of finding who put the knife in.
Best practiceStrain is a situation the players can walk into, not a monologue and not a predicted ending. Prefer deadlines, faces, and next work over adjectives. If nobody acts before the next moon, named people go to work — food, ground, or unity thins. If the sentence could sit on any map, make it local to this place and season. Fail: cropped slogans, a single stabbed-ruler plot that resolves the city.

Name only the people you need to make those strains playable, and how they are tied. Blood and household first — sister, servant of twelve years, children on a named street. Those ties do not reverse. Office, pay, and foreign gold may. A few individuals, not a cast list for a novel.

ExampleDuke Marino Orvanti, wounded, still sitting, brother of dead Lazzaro, husband of dead Isotta. Orsa Solari, Isotta’s sister: she will not let the cathedral bury Isotta as an Orvanti household accident; she wants her sister in the ground as Solari, and she wants to know whether Marino’s household let the knife through. Caterina Vela dressed Isotta for twelve years; her two children live on Mara Vela’s street, which is already on the night roll; she is household, not blood. Captain Vito takes names for Marino because Marino cannot ride. Paolo Cresti copies the roll; he eats from Vito’s table. Piero Calvo holds the stores that feed the lower town; Niccolo is his brother’s son. Agnese Orsani still holds the burgher roll; she stood in the yard when Isotta burned Pelesa’s register. Father Lovro holds the body and the door. Luca Bandi (Pelesa) and Matteo Rinaldi (Osvaldo Calvaro’s purse) rent houses; they want opposite things. Jakov Bracco sleeps in the Company warehouse; Lazzaro stood godfather to his daughter. Tomaso Fero still works the mill behind the hill gate because Lazzaro pulled him off a Pelesan bench twelve years ago and the last order Lazzaro spoke to him was to finish the counterweight engine there. If two named faces have no tie, the notes are not done.
Best practicePrep people with leverage, goals, and ties — not ranks to recite. For each: what they want this season, what they will not forgive, what they do if left, who they are bound to. A faction is a people-group, hue-coded. Categories in this city: blood houses (Orvanti, Solari, Calvo); armed (household arms, Free Company); civic claim (those who hold the duke’s naming, those who still hold the burgher roll); foreign (Pelesa, Osvaldo Calvaro’s purse); faith (the cathedral). Dual membership only across categories: Caterina is Solari household and Mara Vela’s street; Jakov is Company and unpaid Orvanti service. Never two houses of the same category on one person unless the fiction says they changed. Fail: a list of jobs; an object as a faction; two named faces with no tie.

Do not pre-solve the social map for the players. Who is trusted, who leads, who is owed — that is best discovered in play through action, rumour, and consequence. You may hold the blood map in private notes. You do not open with a lecture on who sits where.

ExampleYou know Orsa will not bury until she knows whether Marino’s household let the knife through, and that Agnese will read the burgher roll if the night roll keeps naming streets. You do not tell the table the order of standing. They learn it when someone is obeyed in Marino’s name — or is not — and when a roll is read aloud or a burial is refused.
Best practiceConnections, standing, and hierarchy rungs emerge from fiction. Naming every rung of power up front is a lore dump. Setup names at most five hierarchy axes and leaves the rungs empty of names. A live hall that already has occupancy is in play — a different artifact, pointed at from Hierarchies, not dumped in session zero. Hold what you need for consistency; reveal through scenes.

Three or four visible marks of recent history — things anyone can point at without a speech. Secrets are facts, not a reveal scene.

ExampleBlood scrubbed from the cathedral floor and still in the grain of the stone; Isotta’s body behind the rood screen. Boards and nails stacked against Tomaso’s mill behind the hill gate. A night-roll copy with Mara Vela’s street on it, in Paolo Cresti’s hand. Pelesan hulls at first light across the bay. Calvaro seal-wax on a charter in Matteo’s rented house. Three empty places at the pans.
Best practiceShow, do not lecture. When players ask “what happened here?”, answer with what they can walk to and touch. Repeat marks across sessions until the table has changed them. A secret is a short fact, not a delivery script. Fail: fresco-of-the-week; a cutscene that names who paid for the knife.

At least three lives already in motion. Each is a situation: a want, a next action, more than one way in. Threads may contradict. Alliances reverse. Participation is the table’s. A thread in motion is not a Foundation Myth; a Myth is a thread that already ended.

ExampleVito’s household arms take names at night because Marino cannot ride. Paolo Cresti is already copying Mara Vela’s street onto that roll; people whose names have been on it a week have stopped being seen at the pans. That is how this city now settles who is an enemy — not a banner, an office with a clerk.

Agnese Orsani still holds the burgher roll from when Aspalath named a count. She stood in the cathedral yard when Isotta burned Pelesa’s register of rower-families. She will read the roll in the square if the household keeps naming streets instead of finding who put the knife in. That reading would put the old houses back into the naming of ships, and take it out of Vito’s hand.

Luca Bandi, consul for Pelesa, is buying the Company’s winter so Aspalath cannot close the harbour against Pelesan hulls. Matteo Rinaldi, Osvaldo Calvaro’s man, is buying a client charter so the same harbour chokes Pelesa. They are not allies. Jakov has not taken either purse because Lazzaro stood godfather to his daughter, who sleeps in the warehouse those purses would buy.

Orsa will not let Lovro bury Isotta as an Orvanti accident. Tomaso still works the mill because Lazzaro’s last order to him is unfinished, and Orsa paid his mother’s burial. Piero will sell a cargo to Pelesa if Niccolo will not hold the stores for the quarter. None of these is a required path. Any can rise if the players lean that way — and each advances if they do not.
Best practiceSide stories are not clutter; they are how a campaign feels lived-in. Prep each thread as a situation: people with goals, pressure that worsens if ignored, more than one way in. Do not script which scenes the table must play. If they never take a thread, the world still moves; if they take it, you already know who wants what. Fail: required side quests; theme slogans; one “main plot.”

Set starting Fortunes (Vitality, Cohesion, Surplus, Standing, Tradition) as soft 0–3 from the facts above. Store them when the campaign record is created. Early play may correct them. If you start in play, you may also store 0–3 Foundation Myths that pass the test below, and you name hierarchy axes (rungs empty at setup).

ExampleVitality 1 (wounded duke, dead duchess and brother, night roll already taking hands from the pans — not half the benches empty). Cohesion 0 (private plots are the default: Orvanti household, Solari grief, unpaid Company, old burgher roll, two foreign purses). Surplus 1 (stores still move; a seized warehouse or a wasted sack is a decision). Standing 2 (Pelesa and Calvaro would not buy here if the name were a joke). Tradition 0 (nobody agrees what this city is: the duke’s night roll, the burgher roll, the Company’s warehouse, the cathedral holding the body).

Myths, if in play. A Foundation Myth is a resolved Pivotal Echo, not a founding legend and not the present strain. It passes only if a named person did a named deed, the community joined it, the work ended, someone alive can tag a kind of roll because that memory still governs it, the effect is narrow, and someone alive still hates the deed or still pays for it. Civic slogans fail. The night the knife missed is strain, not a Myth.

Isotta’s Book. Isotta Solari’s dowry included a Pelesan register: Aspalath boat-families listed as owing rower-service on Pelesan galleys, leftover of a war her grandfather lost. The marriage-peace with Pelesa required the book returned. She had it burned in the cathedral yard. The families written in it stood with her and would not let Lovro’s predecessor take the pages to the vestry “for keeping.” House Calvo had used those same names to claim Pelesan convoy-protection on their oil; they wanted the book in a chest, not in the fire. The pages are ash. Pelesa treats the burning as theft of a public instrument and has not sent a peace-envoy since. Aspalath treats the names as free of galley-service. Tag a roll when the work is a list, a register, or a foreign claim on a name. Free extra Exertion (pool untouched). Disadvantage on the other side when they enforce a written foreign claim inside Aspalath.

Lazzaro’s Charter. Twelve years ago Pelesan marines tried the hill gate while their galleys held the harbour. The Free Company, whom Lazzaro had hired and could no longer pay in coin, held the gate. Lazzaro promised them the old Orsani warehouse inside the walls as a barracks in place of the season’s pay. Marino’s father signed rather than lose the gate. The assault ended that week; the house was handed over; Pelesa withdrew. Burghers who still hold the old roll call that house a foreign garrison. The Company still sleeps there. Tag a roll when the work is whether the Company has a right to stand in the city, or to be paid in lodging versus coin. Advantage for Company members on Arms inside the walls. Forced extra Exertion for an Orvanti household roll that tries to expel or disarm them.

Tags are ST marks on a person: favours, hooks, witnessed deeds, debts of life — not objects and not categories. Fail: “Foreign gold,” “Witness,” “Loading crane.” Pass: “Hid Orsa’s cousin the morning after the knife.” “Vito has her children’s names on the night roll.” “Piero stood for him when the Company came for the warehouse keys.” “Lazzaro’s last spoken order to him was to finish the mill behind the hill gate.” “Saw Matteo’s man put a purse in a Solari groom’s hand before dawn.” “Owes Matteo the life of his brother, taken off a Pelesan bench.”
Best practiceFortunes are community weather for every scene — PCs and NPCs. Derive them from material facts. If the board looks comfortable after a killing in the palace and a night roll in the lower town, re-check the strain list; at least one Fortune should be in Crisis when the community is eating itself. Myths: if you cannot name the deed, who joined it, how it ended, and who still hates it, it is not a Myth. Tags: if it is not a fact about a person, it is not a tag.

What the Players Receive

Section titled “What the Players Receive”
  • The ranked list of campaign seeds (examples or your own)
  • The three system constraints stated once

Nothing else.

When seeds are chosen and the community frame exists, proceed to Character Creation.
