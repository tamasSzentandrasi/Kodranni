# Canon

Text has the edge. Video is not the school.

Use a source only for the **take**. If the take contradicts `SKILL.md` locks, drop the source. Verbatim lines live in `quotations.md` (`Q-…`). This file is take, refuse, and availability only.

**Availability:** `free` = fetch the URL if you need more than the stored quote. `print` = paraphrase + section; do not invent a quotation.

## Situation prep (method)

**Justin Alexander — “Don’t Prep Plots” (2009) and *So You Want to Be a Game Master***
Availability: free essays. Quotes: Q-ALEX-SIT, Q-ALEX-DEF, Q-ALEX-DIRTY, Q-ALEX-TOOL, Q-ALEX-3CR.
Take: a plot is a sequence of events; a situation is circumstances plus people with goals. Prep a toolkit (people, places, gear, information), not contingencies. Goal-oriented opponents. Three Clue Rule for any conclusion the table must be able to reach. Status-quo design: write what happens if the PCs never arrive.
Refuse: dungeon/node machinery, published-module habits, “Awesome Town” as an excuse to keep a railroad.

**Vincent Baker — *Apocalypse World* fronts**
Availability: print. Quotes: Q-AW-AGENDA, Q-AW-SCARCITY.
Take: name a fundamental scarcity; give threats a want; write what they do if no one stops them (clock / grim portents). Play to find out. The home front is the community eating itself.
Refuse: custom moves, playbooks, and any supernatural threat type. Do not print “front” or “clock” on the Guidebook page unless the author wants the word. Teach the behaviour in Kodranni language (strain now; what fails if nobody acts).

**D. Vincent Baker — *Dogs in the Vineyard* town creation**
Availability: print. Quotes: Q-DOGS-CHAIN (paraphrase).
Take: a community under escalating pressure, built as a chain, not a cast list. Someone claims more than they are owed → someone is wronged → someone breaks the local rule → the place starts to fail as a place. Do not pre-decide what the visitors should do.
Refuse: demons, false doctrine as cosmology, the Dogs as inquisitors. The ladder is a **pressure chain**. In Kodranni it lands on stores, rights, fords, captives, unpaid blood-price — never on the supernatural.

**Jeremy Strandberg — *Stonetop***
Availability: print. Quotes: Q-STONETOP (paraphrase).
Take: the steading is the campaign. Characters matter because the place does. Improvements and threats hit the same sheet.
Refuse: the fantasy kit (the Stone, gods, steading improvements as magic). Keep the identity: community as the real protagonist.

**John Harper — *Blades in the Dark*; Stras Acimovic / John LeBoeuf-Little — *Band of Blades***
Availability: print. Quotes: Q-BLADES (paraphrase).
Take: factions have clocks; a score is a situation with a target and a cost; the company (legion, crew) is a character. Winter and the road can end you.
Refuse: the scoundrel/heist tone, flashbacks-as-default, undead hordes. Clocks stay in the ST’s notes.

**Kevin Crawford — *Worlds Without Number* (tags); *Wolves of God*; *An Echo, Resounding***
Availability: WWN free edition is fetchable; Wolves / Echo are print. Quotes: Q-CRAWFORD-TAG (paraphrase).
Take: a place is a handful of tags that each give enemies, allies, complications, things, places. Procedures beat a bible. *Wolves of God* is the right historical register (early medieval, human). Domain play is about what a holding can feed and field, not banners.
Refuse: latter-earth science-fantasy, spell tags, random tables used as a substitute for the nine steps.

**Mike Shea — *Return of the Lazy Dungeon Master***
Availability: free essays (CC BY-NC). Quotes: Q-SHEA-SEC.
Take: secrets and clues are short sentences **abstracted from how they are found**. Strong start = something is already happening. Outline potential scenes; throw them away. Prep to improvise.
Refuse: D&D monster/treasure steps, “fantastic locations” as spectacle, ten-secrets-every-session cargo cult. In Campaign Setup this is step 7 (visible marks) and the leave-blanks rule.

**Greg Stafford — *King Arthur Pendragon* (manor, winter phase)**
Availability: print. Quotes: Q-PENDRAGON (paraphrase).
Take: the year has a hard season. Between adventures the holding must be fed, married, buried, accounted. The campaign outlives a knight.
Refuse: Arthurian destiny, passions as a mechanic to import, the Great Pendragon Campaign as a plot.

**Jonathan Tweet / Mark Rein-Hagen — *Ars Magica* (covenant)**
Availability: print. Quotes: Q-ARSMAGICA (paraphrase).
Take: the house is the long character. Seasons of a community (founding, growth, decay).
Refuse: the Gift, vis, hermetic law. The covenant pattern is the Kodranni community sheet.

## Material life (how they stay alive)

If the ST cannot say how this people eat, shelter, and settle quarrels through the hard season, the world is hollow. These sources exist to fail that test honestly.

**Bret Devereaux — ACOUP, “Resources for World-Builders”** (`https://acoup.blog/resources-for-world-builders/`)
Availability: free. Quotes: Q-ACOUP-AG, Q-ACOUP-FARM, Q-ACOUP-FORAGE, Q-ACOUP-FREM.
Take, in this order of use:

- Bread / clothing / iron series: most people farm; cloth and metal are seasons of work, not flavour.
- Logistics / foraging: an army is a moving famine. Conquerors who “just take the harvest” still need hands who know the fields.
- Fremen Mirage: do not write the morally and martially superior savage. Hard living does not mint better people. Maps onto Vardmark without turning them into noble wolves.
- Lonely Cities: the human terrain around a town is fields, not wilderness wallpaper.
- Oaths: practical trust devices, not empty ritual.
- Practical polytheism: people believed their own religion. Kodranni has no magic — religion is still practice, obligation, and calendar, never spells.

Refuse: using ACOUP to write a lecture on milling or bloomery on the Guidebook page. One true constraint per step is enough.

**Ian Mortimer — *The Time Traveller’s Guide to Medieval England***
Availability: print. Quotes: Q-MORTIMER (paraphrase).
Take: a day, a road, a market, a court, a night’s lodging. Sensory and legal, not encyclopedic.

**Frances and Joseph Gies — *Life in a Medieval Village***; **Barbara Hanawalt — *The Ties That Bound***; **Christopher Dyer — *Everyday Life in Medieval England***
Availability: print. Quotes: Q-VILLAGE (paraphrase).
Take: household, manor court, hay, driftwood rights, who is free in which season. The Iceland and Debatable Land seeds already want this grain.

**Fernand Braudel — *The Structures of Everyday Life***
Availability: print. Quotes: Q-BRAUDEL (paraphrase).
Take: the floor under events — food, clothing, housing, money, the limits of speed. Longue durée is weather. Do not turn it into a thesis.

**James C. Scott — *The Moral Economy of the Peasant*; *Weapons of the Weak*; *Seeing Like a State*; *The Art of Not Being Governed*; *Against the Grain***
Availability: print; two essays witnessed at the Anarchist Library. Quotes: Q-SCOTT-BLIND, Q-SCOTT-LEGIBLE.
Take: subsistence ethics (what a levy that threatens next year’s seed *means*); everyday resistance; the state sees grain and bodies, not people; hills and marshes as places people go to stay uncounted; early states as hungry and brittle. Directly usable for Karadağ, Vardmark captives, Ordmouth gates.
Refuse: importing Scott’s politics as the game’s message. Use the mechanisms.

**Ibn Khaldun — *Muqaddimah* (ʿasabiyyah)**
Availability: public-domain Arabic; English via Rosenthal (print). Quotes: Q-KHALDUN-LUX.
Take: a following holds together by shared hardship and claim; luxury and a taken city loosen it in a few generations. Maps onto Fortunes: Cohesion, Tradition, Surplus, Standing. Vardmark at the Bend is this cycle in one season.
Refuse: a printed theory box. Derive Fortunes from the facts; do not lecture ʿasabiyyah.

**Marc Bloch — *Feudal Society***
Availability: print. Quotes: none stored; trained paraphrase only.
Take: bonds, not org charts. Who owes whom, and what happens when the lord is far.

## Weather of an age (not misery tourism)

**Barbara Tuchman — *A Distant Mirror***
Availability: print. Quotes: Q-TUCHMAN (paraphrase).
Take: a bad century as climate, war, tax, and plague *together*. Refuse: piling calamities until the seed is a horror list.

**Emmanuel Le Roy Ladurie — *Montaillou***
Availability: print. Quotes: Q-MONTAILLOU (paraphrase).
Take: one village is enough world. Names, grudges, who sleeps where.

**Icelandic sagas (especially *Njáls saga*)**
Availability: public-domain texts; many translations. Quotes: Q-SAGA (paraphrase).
Take: hay, law, blood-price, followings, the Althing as a deadline. Already in the Iceland seed. Refuse: saga-voice pastiche on the Guidebook page.

**Arnold J. Toynbee** is already quoted in the Introduction. Do not add more civilizational sermon.

## Anti-worldbuilding (economy of line)

**M. John Harrison — “I’m not against worldbuilding…” (2012) and the earlier “very afraid” notes**
Availability: free. Quotes: Q-HARRISON-CLOMP, Q-HARRISON-SENT.
Take: a world can be built in a sentence. Over-engineered, under-designed setting bibles get away from life. Visible marks and two spoken sentences beat a gazetteer. This is the literary form of “leave blanks” and “do not invent worldbuilding.”
Refuse: using Harrison to strip *procedure*. Kodranni still needs the nine steps. Economy is in the *showing*, not in having no method.

## Video (only these)

No worldbuilding-advice playlist is in canon. If video is used at all:

| Source | Use for | Do not use for |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| Historia Civilis | How assemblies, armies, and offices actually behave | Maps, “culture building,” Rome-as-D&D |
| Fall of Civilizations (Paul Cooper) | Weather of a people after the hinge | Lore dumps, collapse-as-aesthetic |
| Modern History TV (Jason Kingsley) | Horses, kit, a day’s travel, what mud costs | Social history lectures beyond kit |
| Sly Flourish (eight steps / secrets / minimum prep only) | Short secrets; strong start; throw scenes away | Setting bibles, monster/loot steps |
| Matt Colville, *Running the Game* — villain-as-person, don’t-overprep only | People with leverage and goals | His worldbuilding playlist, hexcrawls, D&D cosmology |

Prefer the written form of Shea and Alexander over their videos. Prefer ACOUP’s blog over readings of it.

## Not canon

Wrede questionnaires. Sanderson lectures. Hello Future Me. Artifexian. World Anvil method posts. Mythcreants. *Kobold Guide to Worldbuilding* as an authority. Extra History / OSP as method. Shadiversity / Metatron as social history. Any “how I built my 400-page setting.”
