# Quotations

Verbatim lines the agent may quote. Status `verified` means the wording was checked against the locator. Status `paraphrase` means cite the work and restate — never wrap the restatement in quotation marks.

Apply with a `Q-…` id. Do not put these words on the Guidebook page unless the author asks for an epigraph.

## Situation prep

### Q-ALEX-SIT
- **Source:** Justin Alexander, “Don’t Prep Plots,” *The Alexandrian*, 23 March 2009
- **URL:** https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/4147/roleplaying-games/dont-prep-plots
- **Status:** verified
- **Maps to:** W4, W5, W8, anti-plot
- **Quote:** “Don’t prep plots, prep situations.”
- **Apply:** A step that tells the table what happens next is a plot. A step that names circumstances and who wants what is a situation.

### Q-ALEX-DEF
- **Source:** same
- **Status:** verified
- **Maps to:** S1, W4
- **Quote:** “A plot is the sequence of events in a story.” / “A situation, on the other hand, is merely a set of circumstances. The events that happen as a result of that situation will depend on the actions the PCs take.”
- **Apply:** Reject a seed that is already a sequence (“then they will besiege…”).

### Q-ALEX-DIRTY
- **Source:** same
- **Status:** verified
- **Maps to:** E2
- **Quote:** “The dirty secret, though, is that it’s actually a lot more difficult to prep plots than situations.”
- **Apply:** Prefer a shorter gate to a longer contingency lecture.

### Q-ALEX-TOOL
- **Source:** same, section “Know Your Toolkit”
- **Status:** verified
- **Maps to:** W5, W7
- **Quote:** “Typical ‘tools’ include personnel, equipment, physical locations, and information.”
- **Apply:** Who holds it + what they can see = toolkit. Not a cast list and not a delivery script.

### Q-ALEX-3CR
- **Source:** Justin Alexander, “Three Clue Rule,” *The Alexandrian*, 8 May 2008
- **URL:** https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/1118/roleplaying-games/three-clue-rule
- **Status:** verified
- **Maps to:** W7
- **Quote:** “For any conclusion you want the PCs to make, include at least three clues.”
- **Apply:** Visible marks are facts the table can reach more than one way. Do not script the discovery. Do not print “Three Clue Rule” on the page.

### Q-AW-AGENDA
- **Source:** D. Vincent Baker, *Apocalypse World*, MC agenda
- **Status:** verified (wording stable across 1e/2e quotations and the MC playbook)
- **Maps to:** W4, W8
- **Quote:** “Play to find out what happens.”
- **Also:** “Make Apocalypse World seem real.” / “Make the players’ characters’ lives not boring.”
- **Apply:** Strain now and threads worsen if ignored. Do not print “front” or “clock.” Teach the behaviour.

### Q-AW-SCARCITY
- **Source:** *Apocalypse World*, fronts — “Underlying every front is a fundamental scarcity.”
- **Status:** verified (MC materials; Hunger, Fear, Ambition among the listed scarcities)
- **Maps to:** W3, W4
- **Quote:** “Underlying every front is a fundamental scarcity.”
- **Apply:** What must not fail is a scarcity with a face and a deadline, not a theme slogan.

### Q-SHEA-SEC
- **Source:** Mike Shea, “Revealing Secrets,” SlyFlourish.com, 4 March 2019; definition from *Return of the Lazy Dungeon Master*, ch. 6
- **URL:** https://slyflourish.com/revealing_secrets.html
- **Status:** verified
- **Maps to:** W7
- **Quote:** “Secrets and clues are single short sentences that describe a clue, a piece of the story, or a piece of the world that the characters can discover during the game. You don’t know exactly how the characters will discover these clues.”
- **Apply:** Step 7 is the fact, not the delivery. License on the essay is CC BY-NC 4.0; keep quotes short.

### Q-DOGS-CHAIN
- **Source:** D. Vincent Baker, *Dogs in the Vineyard*, town creation
- **Status:** paraphrase
- **Maps to:** W3, W4, W5
- **Paraphrase:** A town is built as an escalation: someone claims more than they are owed → someone is wronged → someone breaks the local rule → the place begins to fail as a place. The GM does not decide what the visitors should do.
- **Apply:** Use as a pressure chain on stores, rights, fords, captives, blood-price. Never as demons or doctrine.

### Q-STONETOP
- **Source:** Jeremy Strandberg, *Stonetop*
- **Status:** paraphrase
- **Maps to:** S3, W9
- **Paraphrase:** The steading is the campaign. Threats and improvements hit the same community the characters live in.
- **Apply:** Community as protagonist. Refuse the fantasy kit.

### Q-BLADES
- **Source:** John Harper, *Blades in the Dark*; Stras Acimovic & John LeBoeuf-Little, *Band of Blades*
- **Status:** paraphrase
- **Maps to:** W4, W8
- **Paraphrase:** Factions have clocks. A score is a situation with a target and a cost. The company is a character. Winter and the road can end you.
- **Apply:** “What happens if nobody acts” in ST notes. Clocks stay off the Guidebook page.

### Q-CRAWFORD-TAG
- **Source:** Kevin Crawford, *Worlds Without Number* / *Stars Without Number* tag format
- **Status:** paraphrase (mechanical fact of the free editions)
- **Maps to:** W5, W8
- **Paraphrase:** A tag is not lore. It yields enemies (or friends), complications, things, and places.
- **Apply:** Who holds it + threads can be tagged this way in ST notes. Do not replace the nine steps with random tables.

### Q-PENDRAGON
- **Source:** Greg Stafford, *King Arthur Pendragon*, winter phase / manor
- **Status:** paraphrase
- **Maps to:** W2, W9
- **Paraphrase:** Between adventures the holding must be fed, married, buried, accounted. The campaign outlives a knight.
- **Apply:** Hard season and Fortunes. Refuse Arthurian destiny.

### Q-ARSMAGICA
- **Source:** Jonathan Tweet & Mark Rein-Hagen, *Ars Magica*, covenant seasons
- **Status:** paraphrase
- **Maps to:** W9
- **Paraphrase:** The house is the long character. Communities have seasons of founding, growth, and decay.
- **Apply:** The community sheet, not hermetic law.

## Material life

### Q-ACOUP-AG
- **Source:** Bret Devereaux, “Collections: Bread, How Did They Make It? Part I: Farmers!,” ACOUP, 24 July 2020
- **URL:** https://acoup.blog/2020/07/24/collections-bread-how-did-they-make-it-part-i-farmers/
- **Status:** verified
- **Maps to:** W2
- **Quote:** “agriculture sits at the foundation of every other form of production.”
- **Apply:** If you cannot say how they eat in the hard season, stop. Do not lecture milling.

### Q-ACOUP-FARM
- **Source:** same
- **Status:** verified
- **Maps to:** W2
- **Quote:** “these folks – these farmers – make up the majority of the population of basically all agrarian societies in the pre-modern period.”
- **Also (same essay):** typical small farms cluster around “3 to 8 acres or so.”
- **Apply:** Most people are not at the ford with a spear. Know where they are when they are not “on a job.”

### Q-ACOUP-FORAGE
- **Source:** Bret Devereaux, “Collections: Logistics, How Did They Do It, Part II: Foraging,” ACOUP, 29 July 2022
- **URL:** https://acoup.blog/2022/07/29/collections-logistics-how-did-they-do-it-part-ii-foraging/
- **Status:** verified
- **Maps to:** W2, Vardmark
- **Quote:** “because everything available that can readily move food also eats food, armies are forced to gather food and other supplies locally.”
- **Apply:** Conquerors who “just take the harvest” still need hands who know the fields. An army is a moving famine. Do not write a logistics lecture.

### Q-ACOUP-FREM
- **Source:** Bret Devereaux, “Collections: The Fremen Mirage, Part I,” ACOUP, 17 January 2020
- **URL:** https://acoup.blog/2020/01/17/collections-the-fremen-mirage-part-i-war-at-the-dawn-of-civilization/
- **Status:** verified
- **Maps to:** W1, W2, W9, anti-noble-savage
- **Quote (tenet 1):** “people from less settled or ‘civilized’ societies … are made inherently ‘tougher’ (or more morally ‘pure’ …) by those hard conditions.”
- **Apply:** Refuse this. Hard living does not mint better people. Vardmark are conquerors who do not know the plough — not noble wolves. Do not print “Fremen” on the page.

### Q-SCOTT-BLIND
- **Source:** James C. Scott, *Seeing Like a State* (Yale, 1998), introduction
- **URL (text witness):** https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/james-c-scott-seeing-like-a-state
- **Status:** verified
- **Maps to:** W6, Ordmouth, Karadağ
- **Quote:** “The premodern state was, in many crucial respects, partially blind; it knew precious little about its subjects, their wealth, their landholdings and yields, their location, their very identity.”
- **Apply:** Lords and agents see grain and bodies. The table should not receive a complete map of standing in session zero.

### Q-SCOTT-LEGIBLE
- **Source:** James C. Scott, *The Art of Not Being Governed* (Yale, 2009)
- **URL:** https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/james-c-scott-the-art-of-not-being-governed
- **Status:** verified
- **Maps to:** W3, Karadağ, Vardmark captives
- **Quote:** “The objective has been less to make them productive than to ensure that their economic activity was legible, taxable, assessable, and confiscatable”
- **Apply:** A levy, a garrison, a hostage list — countable things that must not fail. Hills and marshes as places people go to stay uncounted.

### Q-KHALDUN-LUX
- **Source:** Ibn Khaldūn, *Muqaddimah*, trans. Franz Rosenthal (Princeton, 1967/2005)
- **Status:** verified (wording as given in Rosenthal-citing scholarship; do not invent a page if you lack the edition)
- **Maps to:** W9, Vardmark Cohesion/Tradition
- **Quote:** “Eventually group feeling is altogether destroyed. … The greater their luxury and the easier the life they enjoy, the closer they are to extinction.”
- **Apply:** Derive Fortunes from facts. A following that has taken a city and sits on loot is already loosening. Do not print ʿasabiyyah.

### Q-BRAUDEL
- **Source:** Fernand Braudel, *Civilization and Capitalism, 15th–18th Century*, vol. 1, *The Structures of Everyday Life*
- **Status:** paraphrase
- **Maps to:** W2
- **Paraphrase:** Under events sits a floor — food, clothing, housing, money, the limits of speed. That floor changes slowly and constrains what a season can do.
- **Apply:** Weather, not a thesis box.

### Q-MORTIMER
- **Source:** Ian Mortimer, *The Time Traveller’s Guide to Medieval England*
- **Status:** paraphrase
- **Maps to:** W2, W7
- **Paraphrase:** Enter by a day, a road, a market, a court, a night’s lodging — sensory and legal, not encyclopedic.
- **Apply:** Visible marks and how they live. Not a gazetteer.

### Q-VILLAGE
- **Source:** Frances & Joseph Gies, *Life in a Medieval Village*; Barbara Hanawalt, *The Ties That Bound*; Christopher Dyer, *Everyday Life in Medieval England*
- **Status:** paraphrase
- **Maps to:** W2, Iceland, Debatable Land
- **Paraphrase:** Household, manor court, hay, shared rights, who is free in which season.
- **Apply:** Historical seeds already want this grain. Do not add a social-history essay.

## Weather and economy of line

### Q-TUCHMAN
- **Source:** Barbara Tuchman, *A Distant Mirror: The Calamitous 14th Century*
- **Status:** paraphrase
- **Maps to:** S1, W1
- **Paraphrase:** A bad century is climate, war, tax, and plague *together* — not a menu of horrors to stack until the seed is a list.
- **Apply:** One real pressure and a decision that cannot wait.

### Q-MONTAILLOU
- **Source:** Emmanuel Le Roy Ladurie, *Montaillou*
- **Status:** paraphrase
- **Maps to:** W5, W6
- **Paraphrase:** One village is enough world: names, grudges, who sleeps where.
- **Apply:** Few people with leverage. Leave the rest blank.

### Q-SAGA
- **Source:** *Njáls saga* (and the Iceland seed already on the page)
- **Status:** paraphrase
- **Maps to:** Iceland seed, W4
- **Paraphrase:** Hay, law, blood-price, followings, the Althing as a deadline.
- **Apply:** Use the seed’s own facts. No saga-voice pastiche.

### Q-HARRISON-CLOMP
- **Source:** M. John Harrison, “very afraid,” 27 January 2007
- **URL:** https://gwern.net/doc/fiction/fantasy/2007-01-27-mjohnharrison-veryafraid-criticismofsffworldbuildingandtolkiensubcreationtheory.html
- **Status:** verified
- **Maps to:** W6, E2, do-not-invent
- **Quote:** “Above all, worldbuilding is not technically neccessary. It is the great clomping foot of nerdism. It is the attempt to exhaustively survey a place that isn’t there.”
- **Apply:** A gazetteer on the Guidebook page is this foot. Procedure stays; the survey goes.

### Q-HARRISON-SENT
- **Source:** M. John Harrison, “I’m not against worldbuilding…,” 19 April 2012
- **URL:** https://ambientehotel.wordpress.com/2012/04/19/im-not-against-worldbuilding/
- **Status:** verified
- **Maps to:** W7, E2
- **Quote:** “A world can be built in a sentence, but epic fantasy doesn’t want that.”
- **Apply:** Visible marks and two spoken sentences beat a culture essay. Do not use Harrison to strip the nine steps.
